\## Why WeldAdmin Pro Exists



WeldAdmin Pro was created from real-world experience in the engineering and fabrication industry.



The idea did not originate in a software company.



It originated on the workshop floor, where engineering teams, production managers, quality personnel and management face daily challenges coordinating projects, production, quality documentation, procurement, inventory and customer requirements.



Existing software solutions often solve only one part of the problem.



Some manage accounting.



Some manage inventory.



Some manage quality.



Some manage production.



Very few bring everything together into one platform designed specifically for engineering and fabrication companies.



WeldAdmin Pro was created to change that.

\## Our Philosophy



Engineering should drive software.



Software should never dictate engineering.

Artificial Intelligence should assist engineers by providing better information, improved planning and intelligent recommendations while leaving engineering judgement in the hands of qualified professionals.
